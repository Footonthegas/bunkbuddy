About Us

BunkBuddy.in was built by students, for students. We understand how frustrating it can be to keep track of attendance, especially when you are juggling classes, labs, projects, and everything else college throws at you.

Our mission is simple: give NSUT students a fast, reliable, and privacy-focused tool to stay on top of their academics. No clutter. No unnecessary signups. Just the data you need, when you need it.

We take your privacy seriously. Your credentials are encrypted and stored locally on your device. We never store your password on our servers, and we do not sell or share your data with third parties.

Whether you are checking your attendance before deciding to attend a lecture, tracking your CGPA over the semesters, or simply staying organized — BunkBuddy is here to help you make the most of your college journey.
