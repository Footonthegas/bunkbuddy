Frequently Asked Questions

How does BunkBuddy fetch my attendance?

We log in to the official IMS NSUT portal using your credentials, scrape your attendance data in real time, and display it here. The whole process is automated and takes only a few seconds.

Is my password safe?

Yes. Your password is encrypted and stored locally on your device. We never transmit or store your password on our servers. All communication with our servers is encrypted.

Why does attendance sometimes show zero subjects?

This usually happens when the selected semester or year does not match the data available on IMS. Double-check your selections, and if the issue persists, try refreshing or switching to a different semester.

What is BunkBuddy Pro?

Pro is our premium subscription that unlocks advanced features like detailed analytics, priority support, and exclusive tools to help you plan your academics better. You can cancel anytime from the footer.

Can I use BunkBuddy on my phone?

Yes. BunkBuddy works on any modern browser, including Chrome, Safari, and Firefox on both desktop and mobile devices. Just visit our website and log in.

Who do I contact for support?

You can reach us through the Contact Us page, or email us directly at support@bunkbuddy.in. We usually respond within 24 hours.
