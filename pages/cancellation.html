Cancellation Policy

You can cancel your BunkBuddy Pro subscription at any time, no questions asked. We believe in giving you full control over your subscription.

To cancel, simply click the Cancel Subscription button in the footer of this app. Your Pro features will remain active until the end of your current billing period, and you will not be charged again.

Please note that canceling does not immediately remove your access. You will continue to enjoy Pro features for the remainder of your paid period. After that, your account will revert to the free plan.

If you change your mind later, you can resubscribe at any time. We do not offer partial refunds for unused portions of your subscription.
